Questions, answered

How does the waitlist work?

Join for a specific space or for any office or retail space. When one opens, we contact people on the list first by email and text, and you can apply online right away.

How long is the wait?

It depends on the space. Tenants tend to stay for years, so joining early is the best way to get in. typical wait TBC

Can I see a space before I apply?

Yes. Book a short video tour, or meet Darin at the building when he's on site.

Can I lease two spaces together?

Yes. Some offices sit side by side, and several tenants already lease two joined spaces. Mention it when you join the waitlist.

How do I pay rent?

Online through your tenant account, by card or bank transfer. You can turn on autopay and get a receipt every month.

Who handles repairs?

Log in and send a maintenance request with a photo. We'll confirm and schedule the fix, and you can follow it in your account.
